For home, business, studio, and equipment-heavy teams

ProofCoffer avoids patient records and sensitive client data. It focuses on equipment, purchases, services, warranties, renewals, locations, software licenses, and proof files.

More than a receipt drawer

Newer ProofCoffer workflows help users keep records complete, readable, recoverable, and useful when they need to share proof with a store, technician, insurance desk, landlord, office manager, or internal team.

Record completeness Missing-info prompts, next-best-action cards, required purchase or warranty choices, and cleaner record validation help avoid half-finished proof.
Store and service context Save store links, purchase locations with map pins, payment type, technician or installer names, service person details, and scanned-by initials.
Category-aware tracking Consumables adapt to category and item type, including toner, cartridges, filters, batteries, bulbs, subscriptions, vehicle mileage, and equipment hours.
Accessibility and language Dark mode by default, light mode, theme options, VoiceOver labels, larger-text-friendly layouts, high-contrast care, and English or Spanish interface support.
Backup and transfer Create account backup packages for recovery, transfer, or safekeeping, with reminders to store exported proof files only in places the user trusts.
Business-ready reporting Add company details and logo to PDF reports, preview before sharing, print from the report flow, and export grouped records for a category or asset set.

Free to start, Pro when proof matters at scale

Free users can keep up to 5 proof records. Pro plans are designed for unlimited records, stronger export and backup workflows, protected license fields, business PDFs, and higher-volume asset tracking.

Business teams Teams with 6 or more users, multiple locations, or high-volume inventory can contact business@proofcoffer.com for guidance.
